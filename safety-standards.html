SuperSpace Trust & Safety

Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Zero tolerance

SuperSpace has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This includes child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, and any other sexualization of children, whether in profiles, messages, calls, or usernames. Any account found engaging in this conduct is permanently banned and reported to the relevant authorities.

1. Who can use SuperSpace

SuperSpace is restricted to users aged 18 and over. Age is collected and confirmed at sign-up, and accounts found to belong to a minor are removed on discovery.

2. What is prohibited

  • Uploading, sharing, soliciting, or linking to CSAM in any format.
  • Sexualized conversation involving, or purporting to involve, a minor.
  • Grooming behaviour — building trust with the intent of future abuse or exploitation.
  • Using SuperSpace to direct another user to CSAE content or services elsewhere.
  • Any profile, bio, username, or call content that sexualizes a minor.

3. Prevention

  • Identity verification is required before a guide can accept calls.
  • Automated and manual review of reported profiles, messages, and call metadata for CSAE indicators.
  • Every profile and call carries a visible, one-tap report and block control.

4. Detection & reporting tools

Users can report a profile, message, or call directly from the app. Reports involving suspected CSAE are triaged immediately and are never queued behind routine moderation.

  • In-app report button on every profile and active call.
  • A dedicated safety inbox at info.super.bits@gmail.com for anyone — including non-users — to flag suspected CSAE.
  • Immediate suspension of an account under investigation for CSAE, pending review.

5. Legal compliance & reporting to authorities

Where required by law, confirmed CSAE material and account information are reported to the relevant national reporting body (such as the NCMEC CyberTipline in the United States) and to law enforcement, including under India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. SuperSpace preserves relevant records as required to support a lawful investigation.

6. How to report a concern

If you encounter suspected CSAE on SuperSpace:

  • Use the in-app "Report" control on the profile, message, or call.
  • Or email info.super.bits@gmail.com with as much detail as you can safely provide.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or your national child-protection hotline first.

7. Accountability

This policy is reviewed on a regular basis and updated as our detection, moderation, and reporting processes evolve. Questions about this policy can be sent to info.super.bits@gmail.com.
