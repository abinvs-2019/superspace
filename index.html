FAQ

Good questions

How does pricing work?
Each expert sets a per-minute rate in rupees, shown before you connect. A live meter runs during the call and you pay only for the minutes you actually talk, billed via UPI. There is no subscription and no minimum.
Is my call private?
Calls run over secure, encrypted connections and are not recorded unless both participants opt in. How we handle your data is described in our Privacy Policy below.
How do payouts work for experts?
Experts keep up to 80% of call earnings, which accrue to an in-app balance and can be withdrawn to a UPI ID. Exact revenue share and withdrawal timelines are set out in the Expert Terms.
What if the call drops?
The meter pauses when a connection drops, and you can reconnect to continue. Minutes lost to a genuine connection issue may be refunded to your wallet in line with our refund policy.
Do I need to subscribe?
No. SuperSpace is pay-per-minute. There are no monthly plans, membership fees or auto-renewals — you only pay for the calls you take.
