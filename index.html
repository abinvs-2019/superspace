How it works

Slow down. Say something real.

No swiping through a hundred faces. SuperSpace shows you one person at a time, with their photos and the things they wrote, so the first message can be about them.

The bloom

Every chat starts as a bud.

Our logo is a closed bud, because that’s how every connection starts: small, with no pressure. Each day you both write, your chat’s bloom grows. Miss a day and it droops; say hi and it stands back up.

Gifts, never payments

Little gestures. No strings.

Send a coffee when someone made you laugh, or a bouquet when the chat is going well. They’re pictures, like a GIF, and they’re always optional.

  • Talking is always free. Replies are never limited, and no chat is ever paused until someone pays.
  • No cash value. Stickers can’t be cashed out, redeemed or exchanged, and nobody earns from receiving them.
  • No pressure. The app suggests a gift at most once per chat, after a real conversation. Ignoring it changes nothing.
Safety, built in

Real people. Real profiles.

  • Named, signed-in accounts. Everyone signs in with Google or Apple. No anonymous or random chat.
  • 18+ only. We ask for your date of birth and remove anyone underage.
  • Report and block, everywhere. On every profile, chat and call, reviewed by our team.
  • Verified badge. Members with a plan can verify their phone number and show a ✓.
  • Calls stay private. Voice and video calls are never recorded.

Questions

Good to know

Is SuperSpace free?
Yes. Make a profile, read profiles and start conversations with up to 10 new people a day, free. Replies and ongoing chats are never limited. A plan makes new people unlimited and adds voice and video calls, a monthly allowance of stickers and the verified badge.
Who can join?
Anyone aged 18 or over, with a profile that is really them: their own name, their own recent photos and their real age. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and do not let anyone younger join.
Is it random or anonymous chat?
No. Every account signs in with Google or Apple and has a named profile with photos. You choose who to say hi to, after reading their profile. Nobody is ever matched with you at random.
What are the stickers and gifts?
Little pictures you can send in a chat: flowers to say hi (always free), and coffees, bouquets, chocolates and teddies that open as a chat grows. They are a fun, optional gesture, like a GIF. They have no cash value, and nobody earns money, vouchers or anything else from receiving one.
Do I have to send gifts to keep talking?
Never. Talking is always free, no conversation is ever paused or locked, and gifts are never required. The app may suggest a gift once, after a real conversation, and you can ignore it.
What does the verified badge mean?
Members with a plan can verify their phone number with a one-time code, and their profile then shows a ✓. It means the number is real and theirs. It is not an identity or background check, so always follow our safety tips.
How do I report or block someone?
Open their profile or your chat with them and tap Report or Block. Blocked people can’t message or call you and disappear from your feed. Our team reviews reports and aims to act within 24 hours. You can also email info.super.bits@gmail.com.
How do I cancel a plan?
Plans renew monthly through the App Store or Google Play until you cancel. Cancel any time in your Apple ID subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions; you keep the plan until the end of the month you paid for. See Refunds & Cancellations.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete my account. It removes your profile, photos, messages and matches. If you have a plan, cancel it in your app store too, because deleting the account doesn’t stop store billing.
