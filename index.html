FAQ

Good questions

How does pricing work?
Each expert sets their own per-minute rate for voice and video, shown before you connect. You top up coins in the app over UPI, cards or netbanking (larger packs include bonus coins), and calls are charged per started minute from the moment they connect. Calls that never connect are free. There is no subscription.
Is my call private?
Calls run over secure, encrypted connections and are never recorded. Messages are stored so you can see your conversation history. How we handle your data is described in our Privacy Policy.
How do payouts work for experts?
Experts earn from every call and message. Earnings build up in the app and can be withdrawn straight to a UPI ID. Payouts are reviewed by our team, usually within a few working days. See our Terms of Service.
What if the call drops?
The app tries to reconnect while the call stays open, and the call is charged until one of you hangs up. If it doesn’t come back, hang up and call again. If you lost minutes to a failure on our side, email info.super.bits@gmail.com within 30 days and we’ll credit them back (see our Refund Policy).
Do I need to subscribe?
No. SuperSpace is pay-per-minute. There are no monthly plans, membership fees or auto-renewals — you only pay for the calls you take.
