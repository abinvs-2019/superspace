FAQ

Good questions

How does pricing work?
Each expert sets their own per-minute rate for voice and video, shown before you connect. You top up coins in the app over UPI, cards or netbanking (larger packs include bonus coins), and calls are charged per started minute from the moment they connect. Calls that never connect are free. There is no subscription.
Is my call private?
Calls run over secure, encrypted connections and are never recorded. Messages are stored so you can see your conversation history. How we handle your data is described in our Privacy Policy.
How do payouts work for experts?
Experts earn from every call and message. Earnings build up in the app and can be withdrawn straight to a UPI ID. Payouts are reviewed by our team, usually within a few working days. See our Terms of Service.
What if the call drops?
The app tries to reconnect while the call stays open, and the call is charged until one of you hangs up. If it doesn’t come back, hang up and call again. If you lost minutes to a failure on our side, email info.super.bits@gmail.com within 30 days and we’ll credit them back.
Do I need to subscribe?
No. SuperSpace is pay-per-minute. There are no monthly plans, membership fees or auto-renewals — you only pay for the calls you take.

6. Calls and messages

An Expert’s rates are shown on their profile and on the call buttons before you start. By starting a call or sending a message, you agree to pay that rate.

Voice and video callsCharged at the Expert’s per-minute rate from the moment the call connects, for each started minute, with a minimum of one minute.
Calls that don’t connectNot charged, whether declined, missed, cancelled, or failed for technical reasons.
Dropped connectionsIf the connection drops, the app tries to reconnect while the call stays open, and the call is still charged until one of you hangs up. If it doesn’t come back, hang up. Minutes lost to a failure on our side can be credited back (section 5).
Running out of coinsThe call ends automatically when your coins run out. The app warns you in the final minute, and you are never charged more than your balance.
MessagesCharged at the Expert’s per-message rate when sent. Messages are short (currently up to 50 characters) and are meant for arranging or following up on a consultation.

We never record calls. Calls are encrypted in transit and travel directly between the two devices where possible, or through our relay servers when a direct connection isn’t possible. Messages are stored on our servers so you can see your conversation history. Never record a call or share its contents without the other person’s consent.
