Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how SuperBits Labs, India (“we”) handles personal data in the SuperSpace app and on this website. For privacy questions or requests, contact our Grievance Officer at info.super.bits@gmail.com.

  • Account data: your name, phone number or email (depending on how you sign in), date of birth, gender and profile picture.
  • Expert data: the specialty, bio, rates and photos Experts publish, and what they give us to apply, such as a contact phone number, social media links and, if we ask, verification documents.
  • Messages: the messages you send and receive in the app, stored so you can see your conversation history.
  • Calls: who you called, when, for how long, at what rate and what was charged. We never record the audio or video of calls.
  • Payments and payouts: your coin balance and transactions, payment references from Razorpay, and, for Experts, the UPI ID used for payouts. Full card and bank details are handled by Razorpay, not stored by us.
  • Safety data: the people you block, and reports you make or that are made about you.
  • Ratings and reviews you give after calls.
  • Device and usage data: a notification token so calls and messages can reach your phone, your app version and platform, your IP address (used to protect the service from abuse), in-app usage events and crash reports. We do not collect your location.

  • To provide the service: sign you in, show profiles, connect calls, deliver messages and notifications, and process payments and payouts.
  • To verify Experts, prevent fraud and abuse, and act on reports.
  • To fix crashes and improve the app.
  • To provide support, and to meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Other users see what you put on your profile, your first name and surname initial on reviews, and your name and picture during calls and chats. We share data only with the service providers that run SuperSpace for us, and only for that purpose:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, notifications, usage analytics and crash reports;
  • Apple and Google: when you choose to sign in with them;
  • Razorpay: payments and Expert payouts;
  • Amazon Web Services: storage of profile pictures and photos;
  • the cloud servers that run our backend and call relays.

We also disclose data when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, posts, messages, blocks and devices. We keep call and payment records without your account attached for as long as tax and accounting law requires, and safety reports for as long as needed to protect users. Data travels over encrypted connections, and access is limited to people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure.

You can see and change your profile in the app, and delete your account from Profile → Delete my account or at app.superspace.superbits.in/account-deletion. Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also ask us for access to or correction of your data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance by emailing info.super.bits@gmail.com.

SuperSpace is only for people aged 18 and over. We refuse sign-ups from anyone younger and do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child is using SuperSpace, tell us and we will remove the account. Read our Safety Standards.

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified in the app or on this page, with a revised date above.
