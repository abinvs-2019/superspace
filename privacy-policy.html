In plain words

  • We collect what you put in your profile and chats, plus basic device and usage data to run and protect the app.
  • Other members see your profile. Your chats are seen only by the people in them, and by our team when a report needs it.
  • We never sell your data, show third-party ads, record your calls, or track your location.
  • You can see, change or delete your data, and delete your account, at any time.

1. Who we are

SuperSpace is operated by SuperBits Labs, India (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the SuperSpace app and this website, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. For anything about your data, write to our Grievance Officer at info.super.bits@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

What you give us

  • Account: your name and the email or phone number tied to how you sign in (Apple, Google, or a phone number in some older versions).
  • Profile: date of birth (we show your age, not your birthday), gender, who you’d like to see, photos, prompts and answers, interests and anything else you add.
  • Phone verification: if you verify your number for the ✓ badge, the number and when it was verified.
  • Conversations: the messages, stickers and flowers you send and receive.
  • Calls: who you called, when and for how long. We never record the audio or video of calls.
  • Spotlight: if you apply, your application and our decision, the stickers that count, and the coupons we give you. Tax details only if the law requires them for a reward.
  • Safety: people you block, reports you make, and reports made about you.
  • Support: anything you tell us when you contact us.

What we get when you use the app

  • Activity: the profiles you see, flowers and messages you send, chat levels and blooms, and how you use features, so the app works and we can improve it.
  • Device: device model, operating system, app version, language, a notification token, and your IP address (used to protect the service from abuse). Crash reports when something goes wrong.
  • Purchases: which plan or pack you bought, when, and its status, as reported by Apple or Google. We don’t receive your card or bank details. For coins bought in older versions, the payment reference from our payment provider.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or your photo library beyond the photos you choose to upload.

Sensitive information

Some profile details, such as your gender, who you’d like to see, and anything you choose to share about your beliefs or lifestyle, can reveal sensitive information. You choose whether to share it, and we use it only to run SuperSpace for you (for example, showing you the people you asked to see).

3. How we use it

  • To run SuperSpace: create your account, show your profile, show you people, deliver messages, stickers, calls and notifications, and manage plans and purchases.
  • To keep people safe: check ages, prevent fake accounts, spam and scams, review reports, enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines, and help the authorities when the law requires it or someone is in danger.
  • To improve the app: fix crashes and understand which features work, using usage data.
  • To talk to you: answer support requests and send messages about your account or changes to our terms. Bloom reminders and other notifications can be turned off in the app or your phone’s settings.
  • To meet the law: keep records that tax, accounting and other laws require.

We process your data because you asked us to provide SuperSpace and agreed to these uses when you signed up, and where the law requires or allows it. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account; this doesn’t affect what we did before.

4. Who we share it with

Other members

Your profile (first name, age, photos, prompts, interests and badge) is visible to other members. Your messages are visible to the people you send them to. Your name and photo appear during calls.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run SuperSpace, only for that purpose and under contract:

The law and safety

We share data with the police, courts or regulators when the law requires it, and when we believe in good faith it is needed to protect someone from harm, to respond to child safety concerns, or to protect our members and the service.

If our business changes

If SuperBits Labs is merged, acquired or sells SuperSpace, your data may move to the new owner, who must respect this policy.

We never sell your personal data and never share it for third-party advertising.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, messages, blocks and sign-in. We keep a limited amount for longer only where we need to:

  • purchase and transaction records, for as long as tax and accounting law requires;
  • reports and safety records, to stop people who were removed from coming back and to support investigations;
  • data we are legally required to preserve.

6. Keeping it secure

Data travels over encrypted connections, calls are encrypted in transit, and only people who need access to run SuperSpace have it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a secure sign-in and tell us at once if you think your account has been misused. If a breach is likely to affect you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and change: edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • Delete: Profile → Settings → Delete my account, or the steps at app.superspace.superbits.in/account-deletion.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or your phone’s settings.
  • Under the DPDP Act 2023, you can ask us for a summary of your data and who it was shared with, ask us to correct, complete or erase it, withdraw consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. Email info.super.bits@gmail.com; we may need to confirm it’s you.

If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on our cloud servers and with the service providers above, which may be in India or in other countries. Wherever it is, we protect it as this policy describes and as Indian law requires.

9. Children

SuperSpace is only for people aged 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child is using SuperSpace, tell us at info.super.bits@gmail.com and we will remove the account. See our Child Safety Standards.

10. This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Our host (GitHub Pages) and font provider (Google Fonts) receive your IP address to deliver the pages, under their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change matters for you, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, and update the date at the top.

12. Contact

Grievance Officer, SuperBits Labs, India: info.super.bits@gmail.com. We acknowledge requests within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.
