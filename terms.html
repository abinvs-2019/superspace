Voice and video callsCharged at the Expert’s per-minute rate from the moment the call connects, for each started minute, with a minimum of one minute.
Calls that don’t connectNot charged, whether declined, missed, cancelled, or failed for technical reasons.
Dropped connectionsIf the connection drops, the app tries to reconnect while the call stays open, and the call is still charged until one of you hangs up. If it doesn’t come back, hang up. Minutes lost to a failure on our side can be credited back (section 5).
