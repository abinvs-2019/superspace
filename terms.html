In plain words

  • SuperSpace is a dating app: you browse real profiles one at a time and choose who to say hi to. There is no random or anonymous chat.
  • You must be 18 or older, and your profile must really be you.
  • Talking is free. Plans (billed by Apple or Google, renewing monthly until you cancel) add unlimited new people, calls, stickers and the verified badge.
  • Stickers and gifts are virtual pictures with no cash value. Nobody can sell their time or company on SuperSpace, and nobody earns from gifts.
  • Treat people well. Breaking our Community Guidelines can get you removed.
  • We don’t run background checks. Take care when you meet someone, and report anything wrong.

2. What SuperSpace is

SuperSpace is a dating app for adults who want to get to know each other through real conversations.

  • You choose who you talk to. You see one profile at a time, with the person’s name, age, photos and what they’ve written, and decide whether to say hi. We never connect you to someone at random.
  • Saying hi. A conversation starts when you send someone a hello, usually a free virtual flower on one of their photos or prompts.
  • Chats grow. As both of you keep writing, a chat moves up levels that open new stickers, and grows a “bloom” for each day you both write. These are features for fun and have no value outside the app.
  • Calls. Members with a plan can make voice and video calls to people they are chatting with.

SuperSpace is a place to meet people. It is not an escort, companionship-for-hire, marriage-broking or professional-advice service, and must never be used as one (see section 8).
