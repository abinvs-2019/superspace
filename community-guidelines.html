In plain words

  • Be yourself: your name, your photos, your real age. 18+ only.
  • Be kind, and take no for an answer.
  • Nothing sexual without clear consent, and never anything involving minors.
  • No selling, no scams, and no paying or being paid for company or attention.
  • Report what feels wrong. We read every report.

Our spirit

SuperSpace is for people who want to get to know each other properly: one person at a time, a flower to say hi, and a conversation that grows. These guidelines keep it that way. They apply to your profile, photos, prompts, messages, stickers and calls, and to how you treat people you meet through SuperSpace. Breaking them can get your content removed or your account banned.

1. Be yourself

  • Use your real first name and your real age. You must be 18 or older.
  • Use recent photos of yourself, with your face clearly visible in at least one. No photos of other people pretending to be you, no celebrity or stock photos, and no photos of children on their own.
  • One account per person. Don’t impersonate anyone or pretend to be single if you aren’t.

2. Be respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, insults or threats, and no unwanted comments about someone’s body.
  • If someone doesn’t reply, says no, or blocks you, respect it. Don’t keep contacting them, here or elsewhere.
  • No hate: never attack anyone for their religion, caste, race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or health.

3. Sexual content and consent

  • No nudity or sexually explicit photos on profiles.
  • Don’t send sexual messages, photos or requests to anyone who hasn’t clearly said they want them. Unsolicited explicit images are never OK.
  • Never share anyone’s intimate images, or threaten to.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time, online or in person.

4. Never anything involving minors

We have zero tolerance for anything that sexualises or endangers children. Accounts involved are permanently banned and reported to the authorities. Read our Child Safety Standards.

5. No paid companionship, no scams

  • Never offer, ask for or accept money, gifts or anything of value in exchange for your time, company, conversation, photos, meeting up or intimacy. This includes sex work and escort services.
  • Never ask anyone for money, loans, investments, crypto, gift cards or financial details, however good the story.
  • Stickers on SuperSpace are just pictures with no cash value. Don’t ask anyone to send them, including to reach a Spotlight coupon, and don’t treat them as payment.
  • No gift rings: don’t use other accounts, or team up with others, to send each other stickers.
  • No catfishing, romance scams, phishing links or fake emergencies.

6. No selling or spam

SuperSpace isn’t a marketplace. Don’t advertise, sell, recruit, promote businesses or social accounts, ask for followers, or send the same message to lots of people. No bots, automation or scraping.

7. Violence, danger and illegal activity

  • No threats, violence, stalking, or support for terrorist or violent extremist groups.
  • No graphic or gory images, and no images of weapons used to threaten.
  • No buying, selling or promoting illegal drugs or other illegal goods and services.
  • Don’t encourage self-harm or suicide. If you are struggling, call Tele-MANAS on 14416, free, any time.

8. Respect privacy

Don’t share anyone’s personal information, address, workplace, photos, screenshots of your chat or call recordings without their consent. Don’t record calls without permission.

9. Off SuperSpace

These guidelines follow you when you meet someone from SuperSpace. We may ban people for serious harm that happens offline, such as violence or sexual assault, when we learn about it.

10. Reporting

If something feels wrong, block and report from the person’s profile, your chat or a call. Reports are confidential: the person you report isn’t told who reported them. Our team reviews every report, aims to act within 24 hours, and handles anything involving a child first. You can also email info.super.bits@gmail.com. Please don’t make false reports; that breaks these guidelines too.

11. What we do

Depending on what happened, we may remove content, warn you, limit features, suspend your account, or ban you permanently and stop you making a new account. In serious cases we contact the police. If you think we made a mistake, email info.super.bits@gmail.com within 6 months and a person will review it.
