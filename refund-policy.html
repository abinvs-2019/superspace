Refund Policy

Effective and last updated: 3 October 2026

In plain words

  • Coins you buy are a prepaid credit for SuperSpace. They don’t expire, and purchases are normally final.
  • If something goes wrong on our side, we put it right: we credit the coins back or refund your payment.
  • Email us within 30 days with your payment details and we’ll sort it out.

1. What you pay for

On SuperSpace you buy coins in the app and spend them on calls and messages with Experts, at the Expert’s rate shown before you start. Payments are processed securely by Razorpay. There is no subscription and nothing renews automatically.

2. When you get your money or coins back

  • Payment taken but no coins credited. We credit your coins or refund the payment.
  • Charged twice for the same purchase. We refund the duplicate payment.
  • A call that never connected. Calls are only charged once they connect, so this shouldn’t happen; if it does, we credit the coins back.
  • Minutes lost to a failure of our service. We credit the affected coins back to your wallet.
  • Where the law requires a refund, including your rights as a consumer under Indian law.

3. When purchases are final

  • Coins you have already spent on calls or messages that took place.
  • Unused coins, which don’t expire but can’t be cashed out or transferred.
  • Unused coins when you delete your account. Please use them first.
  • Coins or earnings removed because an account broke our community standards.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email info.super.bits@gmail.com within 30 days of the payment or call, with:

  • the phone number or email address of your SuperSpace account;
  • the payment reference or the date and amount of the payment;
  • a short description of what went wrong.

If we agree a refund, we either credit coins to your wallet, which happens at once, or refund the payment to its original method through Razorpay. Your bank usually shows a refund within 5 to 7 working days of that.

5. Cancellations

There is nothing to cancel: SuperSpace has no subscriptions or bookings in advance. A call can be ended at any time by either person, and is charged only for the minutes up to that point.

6. Purchases through an app store

If a purchase was made through the Apple App Store or Google Play, refunds for it are handled by that store under its own policy.

This policy is part of our Terms of Service. If they ever differ, the Terms of Service apply.
