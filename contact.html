Contact

SuperSpace is operated by SuperBits Labs, India.

Support, refunds and questions

Email info.super.bits@gmail.com. Include the phone number or email address of your SuperSpace account so we can find it.

Report a person or a safety concern

In the app, open the person’s profile or your conversation with them and use Report or Block. Anyone, including people without an account, can also email info.super.bits@gmail.com. Concerns involving a child are handled first. See our Safety Standards.

If someone is in immediate danger, call 112. For a child, call Childline on 1098. For mental-health support, call Tele-MANAS on 14416.

Grievance Officer

For complaints under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, write to the Grievance Officer, SuperBits Labs, at info.super.bits@gmail.com. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and aim to resolve them within 15 days.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account, or follow the steps at app.superspace.superbits.in/account-deletion.
